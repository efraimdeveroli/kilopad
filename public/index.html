Overview

KiloPad is a launchpad for pump.fun coins with one extra rule. Every coin's creator fees are locked to the KiloPad treasury, and when a coin's fees reach $50, they open a 1000x long or short on SOL, BTC or ETH.

If the position wins, the profit buys the coin back from the market and burns it. If it busts, the coin keeps trading and its fees start stacking toward the next $50.

You pick three things at launch: a market (SOL, BTC or ETH), a side (long or short) and a take-profit tier (Degen, Hyper-Degen or Psycho). Those choices are locked in for the life of the coin.

Launching a coin

The launch form asks for a coin name, a ticker, an image, an optional one-line description and an optional initial buy in SOL (up to 5 SOL). Then you choose side, market and tier. A summary box shows exactly what you are committing to before you approve anything.

Your image and metadata are pinned to IPFS first. Then your wallet signs the launch transaction. You connect with Phantom, Solflare or Backpack; KiloPad never holds your keys.

If you add an initial buy, it is a separate second approval after the coin exists. If that buy fails, the coin is still launched.

Launching is paused whenever the treasury address is not configured, so a coin can never be created without its fees being locked.

Fee lock

pump.fun lets a coin's creator fees be split between shareholders. KiloPad launches every coin with a single shareholder: the KiloPad treasury, at 100%, with the admin revoked so the split cannot be changed afterward.

  • One transaction. The launch creates the coin, creates the fee-sharing config and sets the shares to 100% treasury. If that does not fit in one Solana transaction, the page splits it into the fewest steps that fit and asks you to approve each.
  • Verified on-chain. After launch, the server reads the coin's fee-sharing account and only lists the coin in Explore if it pays 100% to the treasury and is locked.
  • Not listed means not locked. If a coin's fees are not locked to the treasury, it does not appear in Explore and its fees never count toward a position.
  • You can check it yourself. The treasury address is shown on the launch page and can be checked on pump.fun or Solscan.

This means creators do not receive creator fees from their own KiloPad coins. The fees are the margin for the coin's position.

The position

When a coin's collected fees reach $50, that $50 is used as margin for a long or short at 1000x. That is a $50,000 position, on the market and side you chose at launch.

At 1000x, a price move of 0.1% against the position wipes out the margin. That is the bust price. The table shows where each demo market busts:

Win: buyback and burn

When price reaches your take profit, the position closes and the profit is split:

  • 80% buys your coin back from the market and burns it. A win means more demand and less supply at the same time.
  • 20% buys back and burns KILOPAD, the platform token.

Example: a Psycho win on $50 margin is $4,950 of profit. $3,960 buys and burns your coin and $990 buys and burns KILOPAD.

Bust

If price moves 0.1% against the position before it reaches take profit, the position is liquidated and the $50 margin is gone. The coin itself is unaffected: it keeps trading on pump.fun, its fees keep stacking, and once they reach another $50 it gets another position with the same market, side and tier.

Explore and fee progress

Explore lists every verified coin with its market, side, tier and a progress bar toward $50. When a coin has reached $50 and is waiting for its position to open, the bar turns full and says so. Latest shows launches as they happen.

Fee numbers are read from the coin's on-chain fee vault. pump.fun pools a creator's fees across all of their coins, so a creator with several coins can see the same number on each until this is refined.

What is live

  • Live: launching coins on pump.fun with fees locked to the treasury, the on-chain check, Explore and Latest, and fee progress toward $50.
  • Not live yet: the bot that opens, monitors and closes positions, and the buyback and burn that follows a win. Positions are not open today.
  • Third party: positions run on Jupiter YOLO, which is an unaudited beta.

This page describes the design. Anything marked not live is not running, and nothing here is a promise that it will be.

Risks

  • Most positions bust. A 0.1% adverse move ends a position. Even the easiest tier is a coin flip before fees and slippage.
  • Profits can queue or never pay out. They depend on the position bot, Jupiter YOLO and on-chain execution all working.
  • You give up creator fees. Fees from your coin go to the treasury, permanently.
  • Smart contract and platform risk. pump.fun, Jupiter YOLO and the wallet and RPC stack can all fail or change.
  • Memecoins are volatile. Burns reduce supply but do not guarantee price.
